Sports and Activities

Sports programmes may be organised according to participants' needs, available facilities, qualified personnel and applicable sport-specific rules.

Para Athletics

Track and field activities adapted to eligible athletes and their individual requirements.

Para Swimming

Swimming activities in suitable, accessible and supervised facilities.

Boccia

A target sport that can offer meaningful participation opportunities for athletes with significant physical impairments.

Wheelchair Sports

Accessible sporting activities selected according to safety, equipment and participant needs.

Inclusive School Games

School-based activities that encourage participation, teamwork and respect among children.

Talent Development

Opportunities to identify sporting interests and support further training through appropriate programmes.

Competitions and Events

Future events may include school-level competitions, district activities, state championships and national-level school sports events, subject to planning, permissions and applicable rules.

Participation and Registration

Schools, parents and interested participants may contact the Federation for information when official registration procedures are announced.

Event-specific eligibility, accessibility arrangements, required documents, fees (if any) and deadlines should be published before registration opens.
