Sports Disciplines

Para Athletics

Track and field activities for eligible athletes, with suitable adaptations and facilities.

Para Swimming

Swimming programmes in accessible facilities with appropriate supervision.

Boccia

Adapted sporting activities based on participants' needs, equipment and safety requirements.

Inclusive School Games

School activities that encourage teamwork, participation and respect among children.

Proposed Competitions

Potential programmes may include school-level sports days, district competitions, state championships and national school sports events, subject to approval, planning and applicable rules.
